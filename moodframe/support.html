Quick Start

  1. Open MoodFrame and allow Camera access.
  2. Choose a look from the style strip or Discover Styles.
  3. Use the guide to compose, then drag the divider to compare the original and styled view.
  4. Take the photo and choose Save Filtered Photo when you want it added to Photos.

Camera Troubleshooting

The camera preview is unavailable

Open iPhone Settings, find MoodFrame, and enable Camera access. Then return to the app. MoodFrame requires an iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

A saved photo does not appear in Photos

After taking a photo, tap Save Filtered Photo and allow Add Photos Only access. You can review this permission in iPhone Settings under MoodFrame.

The live preview feels slow

Close other camera-intensive apps and let the iPhone cool if it is warm. MoodFrame processes the live look on-device, so thermal or low-power conditions can temporarily affect preview speed.

Privacy

MoodFrame processes camera frames and photos on your iPhone. It has no account, analytics, advertising, tracking, or photo-upload service.
